## Cerita (ID)

SI LABU adalah aplikasi React/Vite untuk melaporkan dan berkonsultasi soal perundungan di SMA Negeri 2 Medan. Siswa bisa melapor tanpa login lewat halaman publik yang mobile-first, dengan bukti opsional berupa gambar, video, atau audio, lalu menerima nomor laporan.

Guru BK mendapat portal admin berisi dasbor, tabel dan detail laporan, perubahan status, serta tindak lanjut lewat WhatsApp. Fondasi datanya memakai Supabase dengan aturan keamanan per baris (RLS) dan penyimpanan privat.

## Story (EN)

SI LABU is a React/Vite app for reporting and getting counseling about bullying at SMA Negeri 2 Medan. Students can report without logging in through a mobile-first public page, attach optional image, video, or audio evidence, and receive a report number.

School counselors get an admin portal with a dashboard, report tables and details, status updates, and WhatsApp follow-ups. Its data layer uses Supabase with row-level security (RLS) and private storage.
