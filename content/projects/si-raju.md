## Cerita (ID)

SI-RAJU 2.0 membantu pelajar menemukan minat dan bakatnya, lalu memberi rekomendasi jurusan kuliah yang cocok. Aril juga mempromosikannya sendiri lewat Instagram.

Tes minat bakatnya gratis dan mencocokkan siswa SMA/SMK dengan **216 jurusan kuliah di Indonesia** dalam 8 rumpun, lengkap dengan alasan kenapa sebuah jurusan cocok. Pengguna dipandu **RAJU**, burung hantu 3D orisinal, lewat cerita scroll interaktif dan model minat karier **RIASEC (Holland)**.

- Setiap hasil punya persentase kecocokan dan alasan terkuatnya.
- Seluruh perhitungan berjalan di browser, tanpa daftar akun dan tanpa mengirim jawaban ke server.
- Ada asisten chat SI-RAJU AI (opsional) untuk membahas hasil tes.
- README proyek mencatat skor Lighthouse 100 di semua kategori, dukungan keyboard dan pembaca layar, serta tema terang/gelap.

## Story (EN)

SI-RAJU 2.0 helps students discover their interests and talents, then recommends a fitting university major. Aril also promotes it himself on Instagram.

The free interest test matches high-school and vocational students with **216 real Indonesian study programs** across 8 clusters, and explains why each one fits. Visitors are guided by **RAJU**, an original 3D owl, through an interactive scroll story built on the **RIASEC (Holland)** career-interest model.

- Every result shows a match percentage and its strongest reasons.
- All scoring runs in the browser: no sign-up and no answers sent to a server.
- An optional SI-RAJU AI chat assistant can talk through your results.
- The project README reports Lighthouse 100 in every category, full keyboard and screen-reader support, and light/dark themes.
